About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Sustainable Energy and Environment Thrust at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). I received my Ph.D. from the University of New South Wales (UNSW), supervised by Prof. Zhao Yang Dong and A/Prof. Guo Chen. Prior to HKUST(GZ), I was a postdoctoral researcher at UNSW and the University of Sydney, working with Prof. David J. Hill and Dr. Fengji Luo. My research focuses on smart grid planning and operation, flexible resource optimization and management, and the application of artificial intelligence in energy systems, all aimed at advancing renewable energy integration and grid flexibility for a sustainable future.

Research Interests

My research develops advanced AI, optimization, and game-theoretic approaches for next-generation energy systems.

  • Smart Grid and Flexible Resource Management (EVs, VPPs, eVTOLs, Batteries)
  • AI for Energy Systems
  • Transportation Electrification and Low-Altitude Economy
  • Energy Transaction and Market Mechanisms

Open Positions

I am seeking motivated researchers at all levels with expertise in Electrical Engineering, AI, Statistics, Operations Research/Optimization, Transportation, or related STEM fields:

  • Postdoctoral Researchers: Applicants should have a strong publication record in top-tier journals/conferences and demonstrated research independence.
  • PhD Candidates: We prefer candidates with prior research experience, strong academic potential, and self-motivation.
  • MPhil Students: Opportunities available in smart grid technologies and sustainable energy systems research.
  • Research Assistants: Positions open for outstanding undergraduate and master's students.

Prospective candidates are encouraged to contact me with their CV and research interests.
